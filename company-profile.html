About Ductgrove Limited

Ductgrove Limited is an independent engineering consultancy specialising in the analysis, design, and assessment of pipeline systems, pressure equipment, and industrial ducting. Based in Warrington, Cheshire, UK, we provide practical engineering solutions backed by rigorous analysis and sound engineering judgement.

With expertise spanning dynamic and static analysis, support design, and regulatory compliance, we serve clients across the energy, chemical, pharmaceutical, and general industrial sectors.

Our Expertise

Rigorous Analysis

Comprehensive pipe stress analysis, FEA modelling, and pressure equipment assessment using industry-standard codes and standards.

Practical Solutions

We deliver solutions that are not just technically sound, but practical, buildable, and defendable in the real world.

Regulatory Compliance

Full support for PED (Pressure Equipment Directive), ASME, BS, and other regulatory frameworks to ensure compliance and safety.

Independent Review

Third-party verification and independent engineering assessment for critical piping and pressure systems.

Design Optimisation

Specialist support in support design, spring sizing, bellows selection, and snubber specification for complex systems.

Expert Consultation

Direct access to senior engineers for technical advice, peer review, and specialist problem-solving on your projects.
