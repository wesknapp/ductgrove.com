About Ductgrove Limited

Independent piping, pressure equipment and ducting engineering from Warrington, Cheshire.

Who we are

An independent engineering consultancy

Ductgrove Limited specialises in the analysis, design and assessment of piping systems, pressure equipment and industrial ducting.

Based in Warrington, Cheshire, UK, we provide practical engineering solutions backed by rigorous analysis and sound engineering judgement. With expertise spanning dynamic and static analysis, support design and regulatory compliance, we serve clients across the energy, chemical, pharmaceutical and general industrial sectors.

Clients can draw on senior engineering input and a collaborative approach to technical challenges. We support both larger organisations and smaller businesses, with the scope shaped around the work required.

Capabilities

Our Expertise

Rigorous Analysis

Pipe stress analysis, FEA modelling and pressure equipment assessment using industry-standard codes and standards.

Practical Solutions

Solutions that are not just technically sound, but practical, buildable and defendable in the real world.

Regulatory Compliance

Support for PED (Pressure Equipment Directive), ASME, BS and other regulatory frameworks to ensure compliance and safety.

Independent Review

Third-party verification and independent engineering assessment for critical piping and pressure systems.

Design Optimisation

Specialist support in support design, spring sizing, bellows selection and snubber specification for complex systems.

Expert Consultation

Direct access to senior engineers for technical advice, peer review and specialist problem-solving on your projects.
